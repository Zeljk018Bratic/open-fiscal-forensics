Questions That Deserve Answers

Ako institucije zaista brinu o zaštiti djece — zašto je film koji dokumentuje borbu protiv dječje eksploatacije bio blokiran godinama od strane najvećih streaming platformi i studija? Zašto pitanje odgovornosti za 300.000 djece bez traga ostaje bez sistemskog odgovora? Zašto svako ko javno postavi ova pitanja biva označen, a ne saslušan?

If institutions truly care about child protection — why was a film documenting the fight against child exploitation blocked for years by the largest streaming platforms and studios? Why does the question of accountability for 300,000 untraceable children remain without systemic response? Why does anyone who publicly raises these questions get labeled rather than heard?

Ovo nisu pozivi na mržnju ni teorije bez osnove. Ovo su legitimna pitanja koja svaki roditelj ima pravo postaviti — i koja zaslužuju ozbiljne, javne, provjerljive odgovore.

These are not calls to hatred or baseless theories. These are legitimate questions that every parent has the right to ask — and which deserve serious, public, verifiable answers.
